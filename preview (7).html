Food, Beverage & Nutraceutical Manufacturing

US$382.24BEstimated food processing market, FY2025

India's food processing market has been projected to reach US$689.02 billion by FY2034. Processed foods, ingredients, beverages and nutraceuticals serve buyers across retail, food service, private label and exports.

Marketing focus: product positioning, packaging, product pages and distribution visibility.
Source: industry market estimates; verify the latest report and fiscal-year basis before publication.

Chemicals, Pharmaceuticals & Personal Care

US$30.47BIndian pharmaceutical exports, FY2024–25

Pharmaceutical exports grew 9.4% in FY2024–25. Chemicals, pharma products and personal-care manufacturing serve distinct buyers and need product-specific messaging and careful compliance communication.

Marketing focus: technical product content, capability pages and qualified B2B enquiries.
Source: Indian pharmaceutical export reporting for FY2024–25.

Packaging, Paper & Printing

US$92BProjected Indian packaging industry value by FY2030

Market outlooks have pointed to continued packaging demand from food, FMCG, pharmaceuticals, e-commerce and industrial supply chains. Customisation and application fit are important considerations for buyers.

Marketing focus: catalogues, packaging formats, customisation pages and buyer-specific enquiry journeys.
Source: published Indian packaging industry outlook; confirm the forecast and CAGR with the original report.

Plastics, Polymers & Rubber Products

US$12.5BIndian plastics exports, FY2024–25

India's plastics exports reached approximately US$12.5 billion in FY2024–25. Manufacturers serve packaging, consumer products, industrial applications and automotive supply chains.

Marketing focus: material and application pages, custom manufacturing and export visibility.
Source: Indian plastics export reporting for FY2024–25.
05

Automotive, Agricultural & Mobility Components

US$80.2BAuto component industry turnover, FY2024–25

Indian auto component industry turnover increased 9.6% year on year, while exports reached US$22.9 billion in FY2024–25. OEM sourcing and evolving mobility requirements make capability communication important.

Marketing focus: component pages, technical capabilities, OEM targeting and supplier enquiries.
Source: Indian auto component industry reporting for FY2024–25.
06

Industrial Machinery, Equipment & Flow-Control Systems

US$122.43BIndian engineering exports, FY2025–26

Engineering exports grew 4.86% year on year in FY2025–26. The category spans multiple manufactured goods, not machinery alone. Equipment buyers still need clear information to compare applications and capabilities.

Marketing focus: application-led SEO, equipment pages, technical brochures and quotation requests.
Source: engineering export reporting for FY2025–26.

Electrical, Electronics & Renewable Energy Equipment

100 GWSolar PV module capacity listed under ALMM, August 2025

Listed solar module manufacturing capacity increased from 2.3 GW in 2014 to 100 GW in August 2025. This reflects expansion in the solar manufacturing ecosystem alongside opportunities in electrical and power equipment.

Marketing focus: product specifications, technical resources, installer outreach and project enquiries.

What is digital marketing for manufacturers?

Digital marketing for manufacturers helps businesses improve product visibility, communicate their capabilities, reach potential buyers and generate relevant enquiries. The approach depends on the product, target audience, sales cycle and route to market.

How is manufacturing marketing different from regular digital marketing?

Manufacturing marketing often requires technical product information, application-led content, industry-specific buyer knowledge and longer purchasing cycles. A strategy for industrial components may differ significantly from one promoting a consumer product.

Can digital marketing help manufacturers generate B2B leads?

Yes. SEO, product landing pages, paid campaigns, targeted content and relevant outreach can attract potential business buyers. Lead quality depends on targeting, product-market fit, buyer requirements and how effectively enquiries are qualified and followed up.

How can I market an industrial product online?

Start by identifying applications, differentiators and intended buyers. Build clear product pages with relevant specifications, improve search visibility and create simple enquiry routes. Then test channels suited to the product, target market and sales cycle.

Can Korvion Media help manufacturers find distributors and dealers?

Korvion Media can support digital visibility, targeted campaigns, landing pages and enquiry workflows intended to attract potential channel partners. Distributor appointments depend on demand, commercial terms, regional fit and the manufacturer's ability to serve the market.

Do you work with small and medium-scale manufacturers?

The approach can be adapted to small, medium-scale and growing manufacturing businesses. Priorities may include establishing a credible digital presence, improving product discoverability, generating initial enquiries or building a more structured marketing process within available resources.

Can you help manufacturers expand into export markets?

Marketing can support international visibility, product positioning and outreach to relevant audiences. Export expansion also involves commercial, logistics, documentation and regulatory considerations that must be assessed separately for the product and destination market.

Do all manufacturers need SEO, paid advertising and social media?

No. The right mix depends on how buyers discover and purchase the product. SEO may suit products with active search demand, while targeted outreach or distributor development may be more relevant elsewhere. Channel selection should follow business goals.

Can you help with product packaging and catalogues?

Korvion Media can discuss branding and creative support for packaging, labels, product catalogues and brochures. The scope should be defined around your product, target buyers, brand requirements and applicable compliance needs.
